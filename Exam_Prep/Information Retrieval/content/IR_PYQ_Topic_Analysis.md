# 5. Assumptions & Gaps

- **Unit mapping:** The papers do not print unit names. Units were mapped by the fixed SPPU pattern (Q1/2 → Unit 1, Q3/4 → Unit 2, Q5/6 → Unit 3, Q7/8 → Unit 4). These correspond to syllabus Units III–VI (Units I–II are examined in the in-sem).
- **Unit names** ("Probabilistic Retrieval, Language Models & NLP", etc.) were inferred from the question content and the notes; they are not printed on the papers.
- **Importance rule:** with 4 papers, "more than half" = 3 or 4 papers → ★★★. Questions asked once for 10 marks (2.9, 3.6, 4.5) are ★ by frequency, but are flagged as high-mark questions — prepare them after the ★★★ list.
- **Duplicate printing:** N23 (Q7a/Q8a) and N24 (Q5a/Q5b, Q5c/Q6a) printed near-identical questions twice in one paper; each printing is counted as a raw question but the paper is counted once for frequency.
- **M24 Q5b PageRank graph** is an image in the PDF; it was read visually as: A→B, B→A, B→C, C→B, damping factor 0.7.
- **Study material:** the only IR material in the repo is *Textbooks/IR Sem7.pdf* (62 scanned pages, OCR'd). It is treated as the lecture notes. No separate IR textbook is present, so topics missing from it are answered from standard knowledge and flagged "⚠ Not found in your materials".
